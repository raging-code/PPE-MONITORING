# ADR 0016: node cards, alert cards, chips and selection (P22-2)

Status: Proposed (decided in the P22-2 session; confirm at the next design review).
Design: Rev 3.45 is frozen. Nothing in the frame, the migration, constants.json, alert_catalog.json or ws.schema.json changes.

- **Card model (3.3):** one safety card per responder, its alerts as lines (danger first, then oldest), 3 lines at most, then "+n more" opening inside the card. An unauth_alert is its own dashed card labelled "unverified". An estimate is a slim dashed card labelled "estimate". Quiet cards never use a level colour.
- **Repeats:** unacknowledged rows of the same type on the same responder are one line with a count (the sum of unauth_count, 1 for other rows). An acknowledged row stands alone, so a raise after Acknowledge is a new line. A cleared-but-unacknowledged row and a new live row of the same type join into one line; the line takes the live row's level and is not marked cleared.
- **Node card:** alias, level icon and word, battery and at most 2 badges. Its colour comes from node.level and nothing else. It is a button: a click selects the responder. Last seen, session mode and Start monitoring and End shift belong to the top strip of P22d, so they are not here.
- **Cleared card:** a card whose lines have all cleared keeps its level colour and shows "cleared", so the spike is still noticed. Proposed; design 3.3 gives the label but no colour.
- **Flash:** a card or node card flashes (1 Hz border pulse, steady thick border with reduced motion) while a line is open and unacknowledged. Acknowledge, clear or a quiet card stops it. The 1 Hz pulse is one flash per second, under the 3 per second limit.
- **Minimize:** a card minimized to a chip stays minimized until its signature changes (a newer alert on the card, or a higher level), then it reopens. A new DANGER can therefore never hide behind a chip. The chip text is alias, level word, count and elapsed time (server time, m:ss). Chips sit above the tray until P22d moves them to the top strip.
- **Dismiss:** only estimate cards have Dismiss. A safety or unverified card has Minimize only. A dismissed estimate stays dismissed until reload; the ids are screen state.
- **Selection:** the selected jacket lives in store/ui.ts, which no incoming message writes. A new alert cannot switch it; the node card flashes instead (3.3).
- **Not done here:** Acknowledge and Resolve buttons, `ack pending`, persistence (P22-3); advisory slim card and side panel (P22d); tray height limit, overlap measurement and reduced-motion check in a real browser (P22c Playwright).
