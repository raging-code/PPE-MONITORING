# ADR 0027: AudioThread, self-check, zero-socket warning, fallback pulse and test-alarm (P23b)

Status: Proposed (decided in the P23 session; confirm at the next design review).
Design: Rev 3.45 is frozen. Nothing in the frame, the migration, `constants.json` or the settings keys changes.

- **Shape.** `AudioThread` in `io/audio_watchdog.py` (design 14.5 names no other file). All logic is in `tick()`; `run()` calls it about every 50 ms and `set_items()` wakes it at once. The sound card is a backend object with `play(path, loop, wait)`, `beep(hz, ms)` and `stop()`; `WinsoundBackend` is the real one (stdlib `winsound`), tests use a fake. The clock is injected. One bad tick is counted in `errors` and never ends the thread. `last_tick` is the liveness stamp for the OS watchdog (P24 wires it). P24 also wires `set_items()` (open alerts and active banners, each with `acknowledged`), `sockets_fn` (`Hub.connected_tabs`), `monitoring_fn` and `on_event` (AUDIO_FAULT and AUDIO_OK to `system_events` and the banner).
- **Playing.** Siren loops (`SND_LOOP`) until its last item is acknowledged. beep and one_beep play at once and repeat every `BEEP_REPEAT_MS` = 10 s. chime plays once. A change of the picked sound stops the old one first, so one sound plays at a time. Acknowledged items are silent, so a new unacknowledged DANGER after an acknowledged one starts the siren again. The host sounds from host start; Start monitoring does not gate it (3.12, Rev 3.29).
- **Numbers that are module constants, not settings keys** (the design states them in words only; the keys are frozen): beep repeat 10 s, pulse 1 kHz 400 ms every 2 s, zero-socket 10 s, poll 50 ms.
- **Self-check.** At start and every `audio_check_interval_s`, `check_sounds()` runs again and, while nothing sounds, `probe.wav` plays synchronously. A `RuntimeError` or `OSError` from any play call is the device fault. AUDIO_FAULT is emitted once when the first cause appears, AUDIO_OK once when the last cause clears (a device or a file cause).
- **Fallback pulse.** A WAV that the check marked missing or altered is never played: the pulse sounds instead. An altered file is treated like a missing one because it cannot be trusted to be audible (decision; the design says only that it raises AUDIO_FAULT). A failed play call also switches to the pulse, and the WAV is tried again at each pulse, so a restored device returns to the normal sound. A missing or unreadable manifest raises AUDIO_FAULT but the files still play.
- **15 minute chime.** `host_chime` plays when `host_chime_interval_min` has passed with no safety alert sounding. The count restarts while any rank 2 to 6 sound is playing (the alarm itself proves the path), so the chime comes 15 minutes after the alarm ends, not at once. Ranks 7 and 8 do not suppress it.
- **Zero-socket warning (built, ADR 0026).** With `monitoring_active` and no connected `/ws` tab for 10 s, the host plays `beep` every 10 s. Decision: it ranks as a WARNING (rank 5), so every siren outranks it and a real WARNING does not double it. The design names no sound for it. It stops when a tab connects or monitoring ends; a new disconnect counts a fresh 10 s. No stop rule, as ADR 0026 says. Change the sound or rank in `_want()`.
- **test-alarm.** `python -m ppe_host.cli test-alarm [--seconds N]` runs the manifest check, then plays the siren (default 3 s), the beep and the host_chime, so the operator also hears the 15 minute chime. Exit 1 with AUDIO FAULT if a file is bad or a play call fails. It writes nothing to the database; the result goes in the shift record by hand (3.12).
- **Not here.** `cli ack --node N` needs the running host and stays with P24 (ADR 0009). The real `winsound` behaviour (SND_ASYNC failures, SND_LOOP, `Beep` on a machine with no PC speaker) is the bench audio probe of 3.12 and is not tested in chat. Optional pycaw mute detection (3.12 c) is not built.
