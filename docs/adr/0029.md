# ADR 0029: Start-up steps 6 and 7, session start and the first system rows (P24-2)

Status: Proposed (decided in the P24-2 session; confirm at the next design review).
Design: Rev 3.45 is frozen. Nothing in the frame, the migration, `constants.json` or the settings keys changes.

- **Scope.** `begin_session()` in `main.py` does steps 6 and 7 in one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction; a failure rolls everything back (no session, no row). Steps 8 to 10 (threads, Uvicorn, 1 Hz tick) need the real Source and Port, so they move to P24-3, which makes the split: P24-3 steps 8 to 10 with the Source and Port, P24-4 doctor and the `fw_version` check, P24-5 supervisor and `cli ack`.
- **Overlap with P25 (decision).** The design's step 6 needs a close and a resume rule that P25 also lists. Here only the ABANDONED close is built, inline, because without it a stale open session blocks the new one (`idx_sessions_one_open`). P25 builds the general `close_session(kind)` for ENDED, CLEAN_SHUTDOWN and ABANDONED in `services/recovery.py` and may move this code there; the rows written here are the ones the design specifies, so P25's tests stay valid. P25 also does the rebase of old monotonic stamps.
- **Resume rule.** Resume only if mode, `constants_hash` and `settings_hash` all match and the newest row of that session (over frames, alerts raised or cleared, audit_events, system_events, or `started_ts` if it has none) is younger than `session_resume_max_min`. Otherwise `SESSION_ABANDONED` goes into the old session with detail `mode_changed`, `config_hash_changed` or `stale_30min` (in that order of precedence when more than one applies; the design does not rank them), `ended_ts` is the close time (decision: the design gives no value), and a new session opens with `monitoring_active` 0. Only an ENDED close carries `monitoring_active` forward. For a changed hash the new session also logs `CONFIG_CHANGED`. Alerts and episodes are never touched.
- **Rows of step 7.** `HOST_START` detail is JSON: `host_boot_id` (a new UUID hex), `mono_base_ms` (the monotonic clock at start), and two extra keys that the design does not name, `wall_base_ms` and `mode`, so P25 can rebase old stamps from a (wall, mono) pair. Then `RECOVERING_ENTER`. After a failed quick check: `INTEGRITY_FAIL` (names the quarantined file and the backup used) in the session; in LIVE also the `DB_RESTORED` audit row with `session_id` NULL, written when a backup was restored and also when a fresh database was made (reason `fresh database`); `Startup.host_gap_reason` is set to `db_restored` for the banner (P24-3 shows it). SIMULATOR and REPLAY write no `DB_RESTORED`.
- **event_seq.** The rows take their numbers from `Startup.event_seq`, which advances; Ingest gets the value after step 7 as `event_seq_start`.
- **`host_build`** is `ppe-host <package version>` (the build manifest of P27 may replace it). `fw_version` stays NULL until P24-4.
- **Not here.** `RECOVERING_EXIT`, `HOST_GAP`, the rebuild and a clean-shutdown close (P25).
