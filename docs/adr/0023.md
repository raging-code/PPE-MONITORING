# ADR 0023: CO chart and window layout (P22c-5)

Status: Proposed (decided in the P22c-5 session; confirm at the next design review).
Design: Rev 3.45 is frozen. Nothing in the frame, the migration, constants.json, alert_catalog.json, hotspots.json or ws.schema.json changes.

- **CO chart (3.2):** `components/CoChart.tsx` over `alerts/trend.ts` (pure). One small chart for the selected responder, placed under the component grid inside its own error boundary ("Chart unavailable"; the boundary of `JacketView.tsx` is now exported and takes its sentence). It shows the last 30 minutes of CO in ppm and dotted lines at the warning and danger limits, labelled "35 ppm warning" and "200 ppm danger". The limits are read from the snapshot `constants` (`co_warn_ppm`, `co_danger_ppm`, `co_clamp_max_ppm`, `signal_lost_s`), with the generated constants only as a fallback; no limit is typed in the chart. Tier 1 draws no forecast line. The line is neutral ink: it is a history, not a state, and level colour stays on the cards and the grid.
- **Drawn with plain SVG, not Recharts (Proposed):** table 3.8 lists Recharts, but it is not in `package.json`, a patch script cannot write a trustworthy `package-lock.json`, and a hand-written polyline is fully testable in jsdom. Labels are HTML, so they stay 14 px or more. If you want Recharts, say so; the pure `plot()` stays and only the component changes.
- **History:** the snapshot's `co_history_30m` is the start. Each `reading` message then adds one point (`rx_ts`, `co_ppm`) in `store/reducer.ts`, and points older than 30 minutes are dropped, so a 24 h shift never grows the list. A reading with `co_validity` UNRELIABLE adds no point (design 3.2: the display never shows 0 ppm for a failed sensor) and a `duplicate` frame adds none. A new snapshot replaces the list, so a reconnect redraws from the host. While CO is UNRELIABLE the chart says "CO unreliable: no new points on the line".
- **Gaps (Proposed):** two points more than `signal_lost_s` (15 s) apart are not joined: the line breaks, so a silent node never looks like a flat line. The value is a constant, not a new number.
- **Axis (Proposed):** 0 ppm to the larger of the danger limit and the highest reading, never above the display clamp (500 ppm), so the danger line is always on the chart. Readings are drawn at the clamp at most.
- **Layout (3.7):** `Shell` is now one frame the height of the window (`h-dvh`, flex column). Three regions: (1) the header holds the Sessions and End shift buttons, then the banner stack (TEST or REPLAY bar, system banners, sound bar), then any open Sessions or summary panel; it is capped at **35% of the window height** and scrolls inside itself; (2) the readings area (cards, grid, chart) takes the rest, never less than the 25% left over, and scrolls inside itself; (3) the tray is pinned at the bottom, capped at **40%** (design 3.7) and scrolls inside itself, danger first. An empty tray takes no space. Banners are listed DANGER first (ADR 0021), so a scroll in a full header never hides a danger banner first. The **35% is my number** (the design gives only the 40%): it keeps at least 25% of the window for the readings when everything above is full. Say if you want another value.
- **Chips:** the chips of minimized alerts moved out of the scrolling readings area into a thin strip above the tray, so a minimized danger alert is never scrolled out of sight. They join the top strip in P22d.
- **Sessions list:** it no longer scrolls inside the header (no scroll inside a scroll); the header scrolls. It is still in normal flow and covers nothing.
- **Design finding, not changed:** design 3.6 says every colour pair meets 4.5:1 ("checked"). Measured: stale text #6b7280 on the stale fill #e5e7eb is **3.90:1**. The other pairs pass (normal 5.02, warning 11.58, danger 6.47, ink on white 17.74, stale text on white 4.83). It is shown on the grey "Not connected" card, the "unreliable" grid rows, the NO DATA level and the "estimate" label. Proposed fix: text #4b5563 (6.10 on #e5e7eb, 7.56 on white). The colours are design values, so they are not changed here; `a11y_static.test.ts` carries an expected failure (`it.fails`) that starts passing when they are.
- **Not here:** the side panel with its 8 reading rows is P22d. This stage keeps a readings area that can no longer be pushed off screen; the row count is P22d's test. Playwright rows (at 1366x768 and 150% zoom with every banner active, the danger cards and readings are on screen and the tray is at most 40% of the height; the wake lock is re-acquired after hide and show; flashing under 3 per second in a real page) need a real browser.
