# ADR 0001: advisory window_s and the 30 s evaluation cadence

Status: Proposed (decided by the project owner in the P9 session: "you decide"; confirm at the next design review).
Design: Rev 3.45 is frozen. Section 14.3 and Migration 0001 (`advisory_summaries.window_s INTEGER NOT NULL`) need a window value, and 14.3 names a 30 s evaluation cadence, but neither `shared/constants.json` nor `host/config/settings.json` has a key for either. `advisory_heartbeat_s = 300` exists in settings and is used as is.

Decision: two module constants in `host/ppe_host/core/advisory.py`, not new keys, so the frozen constants and settings files stay unchanged.

- `ADVISORY_WINDOW_S = 60`: the seconds of history one advisory row covers. It is also the base of the quality rule of 14.3: coverage is the evaluated frames seen in the last 60 s divided by 60 / `routine_period_s` (30 frames at the 2 s period). High needs no fault and at least 80 % (24 frames), medium no fault and 50 to under 80 %, low any fault or under 50 %.
- `ADVISORY_EVAL_S = 30`: the "every 30 s per active node" of 14.3.

Why 60 s: long enough that the 50 % and 80 % thresholds mean something, equal to the 60 s windows the design already uses (`link_lost_max_s` in Profile A, `sensor_fault_danger_window_s`), and short enough that a change shows within a minute. 300 s would hide a change; 30 s would leave only 15 frames.

Consequences: Profile B (`routine_period_s` 8) gives 7 or 8 frames per 60 s window, so its quality is at best medium until a later ADR sets a profile window. A node first seen less than 60 s ago has an incomplete window, so its first rows are quality low. If the owner prefers real keys, move both values to `constants.json`, regenerate, and delete the two module constants; `AdvisoryConfig` already carries them as fields.
