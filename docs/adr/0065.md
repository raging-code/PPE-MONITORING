# ADR 0065: The speed selector of a REPLAY dashboard (S7-7b)

Status: Proposed (confirm at the next design review). Dashboard side of ADR 0064; no host, wire, endpoint, migration or constants change.
Design: Rev 3.45 stays frozen. The dashboard words are added to the REPLAY speed addendum at the end of `docs/design/s03-8.md`. The look follows 3.7 (the TEST or REPLAY bar is dark and neutral, never a level colour).

- **What.** The replay bar of ADR 0062 gains a group of three buttons, `×1`, `×4` and `×16` (`role="group"`, name `Replay speed`, `data-testid="replay-speed"`), after the Pause button. The button for the host's current speed is white and has `aria-pressed="true"`; the others are outlined on the dark bar. Each is at least 44 px high and wide. Screen readers say `Speed ×4`. The group exists only where the bar exists: `session.mode` is REPLAY and the host sent the `replay` block.
- **State is the host's.** A press posts `POST /api/replay/speed {speed}` (ADR 0064). On 200 the store takes `{speed, paused, finished}` from the answer at once; the host's system message says the same to every other screen, and a reload reads it from the snapshot. A refusal or a failure changes nothing in the store. The speed list is `REPLAY_SPEEDS = [1, 4, 16]` in `src/alerts/replay.ts`; a Vitest test reads `SPEEDS` from `host/ppe_host/services/replay.py` and compares, and a pytest compares the mock host's list.
- **Presses.** The speed the replay already has sends nothing. A speed can be chosen while paused: the words stay `Replay paused, ×N` and the Resume button stays (ADR 0064 decision C). The speed buttons share one request at a time with Pause: while either request is on its way every control of the bar is disabled, so a double click or a click on the other control sends nothing. All are disabled while the host is recovering and after the end of the data. One sentence under the status for each refusal, the same ones as for Pause; a 404 (a host without the route) says `This host cannot change the replay speed.`
- **Files.** `src/alerts/replay.ts` (`REPLAY_SPEEDS`, `speedChoices`, `choices` in the view, `replayMessage(r, what)`), `src/api/actions.ts` (`postReplaySpeed`), `src/store/replay.ts` (`setReplaySpeed`), `src/components/ReplayBar.tsx`. No new store field.
- **Mock host.** `e2e/mock_host.mjs` gains `POST /api/replay/speed` with the host's answers (200 `{speed, paused, finished}`; 401; 409 STATE_CONFLICT when not REPLAY or finished; 422 for anything but the whole numbers 1, 4 and 16), a system message to every signed-in data socket when the speed changed and none for a repeat, and `replaySpeeds` in `/__state`. It is the mock's own copy of the rules; the real route is covered by `test_replay_speed.py`.
- **Decisions to confirm.** (1) Three buttons, not a dropdown or a stepper: one press, no hidden state, and the host's list is short. (2) The chosen speed is shown by colour and by `aria-pressed`, and the words also say it. (3) Pause and speed share one request at a time.
- **Checked here (development sandbox, Linux, no network).** The mock host's new route was run as a real process (a stand-in for the `ws` package, which is not installed here) and answered 409, 401, 422 for 2, the string 4, true, 4.5 and an empty body, 200 for 1, 4 and 16, no change for a repeat, and a speed while paused; the TypeScript files parse and the pure files show no type errors (react, zustand and node types are not installed here, so the full `tsc` was not run).
- **Not here.** `npm ci`, `tsc`, Biome, Vitest (the two new files, 32 tests, and the one changed line in `replay_ui.test.tsx`), the pytest and the Playwright run (`replay_speed.spec.ts`, 5 tests) were NOT run: no network in the sandbox. A run against the real host, Windows, a touch screen.
