### 3.2 Readings shown

- Battery percent with two distinct thresholds in constants.json: early notice flag at bat_low_pct <= 20% (status2 bit 2, shown on the battery reading row without a pop-up card) and safety alert battery_low WARNING card at bat_warn_pct <= 10%, latched once triggered until the node is placed on charge or power-cycled (Spec), so voltage rebound under lighter load cannot flap the alert. SCBA ON or OFF and the SCBA expired state, always shown (Spec).

- Temperature and humidity, chest motion, and the stillness state against STILL_G, including the man-down state and its 30 s stillness timer. Posture, RSSI and SNR sit in one collapsed Details row (Proposed). The 30 s stillness timer is the backend's estimate from 2 s frames (the frame carries only the raw still_now bit and motion RMS, not the node's own timer), so it is labelled "backend estimate" and the node's own man-down state always wins.

- Link state and last-seen time. Node level: normal, warning or danger.

- CO forecast 5 minutes ahead in ppm and the early-warning badge (Tier 2). The COHb slot stays visible as "unavailable" (decided). Numeric COHb stays hidden until the CFK gate closes and is then shown only as a range, never a point value (Spec). The forecast estimates sensor CO, not COHb, and is labelled as a model estimate, simulated or bench-trained and not field-validated.

- Trend chart (one small chart per responder, decided): it shows CO in ppm for the last 30 minutes, with dotted lines at 35 ppm (warning) and 200 ppm (danger). In Tier 2 the 5-minute forecast continues as a dashed line labelled "estimate". In Tier 1 there is no forecast line. The history arrives in the snapshot (3.8). Details Proposed. The 35 and 200 ppm lines come from the snapshot (generated constants), not from numbers typed into the dashboard.

- Cards show at most two badges in priority order. Wrist disconnected (quiet) is shown apart from wrist lost (WARNING) (Spec). Order (Spec, summary section 8): man-down, MAYDAY, rebooted while man-down; SCBA expired; check-in; sensor fault; stuck button; restarted or restored; shadow disagreement; CO warming or rising; ambient heat; missed event; weak signal; early warning; trend differs; unusual reading. The alert catalog carries unique monotonic badge_order integers (1..32) with deterministic severity-first tie-breaking by (level_rank DESC, badge_order ASC, alert_id ASC) where DANGER=2, WARNING=1, NONE=0, guaranteeing a DANGER badge (such as co_threshold >=200 ppm) is never hidden behind two WARNING badges.
