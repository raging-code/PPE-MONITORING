### 2.5 Export and integrity

- The full CSV export (Spec: the full log; node and time filters are Tier 2) is the view v_full_log (canonical SQL in Section 11) over frames (raw un-clamped co_ppm, temp_c, hum_pct, motion_g, bat_pct, scba_on, mandown, verify, rssi, snr, and raw_hex), alerts (one row on raised_ts ordered by event_seq, and one row on cleared_ts when non-null ordered by COALESCE(cleared_event_seq, event_seq)), audit_events, system_events, and advisory_summaries ordered by event_seq ASC, log_ts ASC. CSV Formula & Hex Protection (Proposed): string/text columns (code, detail, raw_hex) are escaped if starting with =, +, -, @, tab (0x09), or CR (0x0D), and raw_hex is formatted as an Excel-safe text literal (prefixed with ' or exported with explicit text quoting) so all-digit or 'E'-containing hex strings (e.g., 01001234E5...) are never coerced to scientific notation; numeric columns (rssi, snr, temp_c, co_ppm) are emitted as plain unprefixed numbers so negative RSSI (-68.5) is never mangled. CSV export streams in 500-row chunks on a read-only SQLite connection in a worker thread. Audit rows whose session_id is NULL (offline CLI administrative actions, see audit_events) are not part of this view or the CSV export; they stay in audit_events and are read from the table. A per-session export is filtered on the session_id column of v_full_log; without session_id the whole database is exported (2.11).

- Quick check (PRAGMA quick_check) at cold start only, so a restart inside a shift is not delayed; a full integrity check (PRAGMA integrity_check) runs off-loop after RECOVERING ends and at session end, and an online backup (sqlite3 backup API, 250 pages/step) runs off-loop at the end of each session (Proposed).
