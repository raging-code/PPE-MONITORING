### 2.7 Size estimate (confirm in the soak test, see 2.9)

Three nodes at one frame per 2 s (ROUTINE_PERIOD, Spec) is 1.5 frames/s, about 5,400 frames/h. At about 300 bytes per row including the hex copy that is about 1.6 MB/h, or about 13 MB per 8 h shift before indexes. The 1 Hz base_health rows add 3,600 rows/h at an assumed 40 bytes, about 1.2 MB per shift. Total: about 14 MB per 8 h shift. Tier 2 ml_outputs at an assumed 100 bytes per scored frame adds about 4 MB per shift. Event bursts add little. These are estimates, not measurements.
