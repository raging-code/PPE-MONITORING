### 3.13 Dashboard and WebSocket types

- WebSocket messages (snapshot, reading, alert, system) are defined as one Pydantic v2 discriminated union on the "type" field. TypeAdapter(...).json_schema() is exported to shared/ws.schema.json, TypeScript types are generated from it, and CI fails if the generated file differs. openapi-typescript covers REST only, because FastAPI leaves WebSocket payloads out of OpenAPI.

- The flat component grid fallback (3.1) is the shipped Tier 1 jacket view. The 3D jacket is optional (S7) and is built later on the same hotspot data. The missing CAD .stl is therefore no longer a pre-build blocker. Items 9 and 10(1) of Section 9 and pre-build action (a) of Section 10 apply only to S7.
