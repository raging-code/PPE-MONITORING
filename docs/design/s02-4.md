### 2.4 Relationships

- roster (node_id) 1 to many verified frames (verify = OK), alerts and episodes. Diagnostic frames carry an unauthenticated node ID and have no foreign key.

- episodes 1 to many alerts and audit_events.

- operators 1 to many audit_events.

- frames 1 to many alerts (cause); 0 or 1 to 1 ml_outputs (Tier 2).
