### 3.5 Estimates and advisory (Proposed)

- Estimates (forecast, early warning, anomaly flag, COHb range) and advisory text use a dashed border and a grey label ("estimate" or "advisory"). They never use a level colour.

- Advisory text shows as a slim card in the tray and as one line in the side panel (risk and action). Tier 1 uses the fixed advise(level, reasons) table.

- Quality from the advisory rule (2.3) shows as a word and three dots: high, medium or low confidence. Low-quality advice is greyed.
