### 3.3 Alert pop-ups

A pop-up carries sound and a flash. Vibration is not used: a laptop browser cannot vibrate, and the jacket's own buzzer already alerts the wearer (decided, 23 Nov 2026). Every open alert stays on the dashboard until it is dealt with.

| Class | Examples | Sound | Minimize and close |
|---|---|---|---|
| Safety alert (deterministic) | Man-down (the fall and motion alert in Tier 1), MAYDAY, CO, link lost, signal lost, base offline, SCBA expired, sensor fault, low battery, ambient heat | Danger: siren. Warning: beep. Repeats until Acknowledge | Can be minimized to a chip. Cannot be closed until it clears or is resolved. |
| Estimate or advisory | CO forecast, early-warning rule, advisory text, anomaly flag | One soft chime, labelled "estimate" or "advisory" | Can be minimized or dismissed. Never changes a card colour or the level. |

- Several alerts at once (decided): all show together in one tray at the top and none overlaps another. The tray pushes the screen down instead of floating over it, so no reading is hidden.

- One card per responder (Proposed): with at most 3 nodes, the tray holds at most 3 safety cards side by side. Several alerts of one responder share its card, highest level first, three lines at most, then "+n more" opening inside the card. Estimate and advisory notices are slim cards under the safety cards. Order: danger before warning, then oldest first. BASE OFFLINE is a full-width banner above the cards.

- One sound at a time (Proposed + Rev 3.17 Complete Priority List): highest class plays: (1) NO LIVE DATA (repeating siren, 60 s local silence); (2) MAYDAY and MAN-DOWN (siren); (3) BASE OFFLINE (siren); (4) other DANGER alerts (CO >=200 ppm, link lost, node-reported DANGER, DANGER sensor fault, SCBA expired + high CO — siren); (5) WARNING safety alerts and LOGGING FAULT / HOST BATTERY <=30% banners (beep); (6) UNAUTHENTICATED ALERT (beep); (7) RECOVERING and HOST_GAP banners (one beep); (8) Tier 2 estimate/advisory (soft chime). Acknowledge silences that card only, and sound continues while any safety card is unacknowledged. This list orders the host alarm (3.12); item (1) is the browser siren. AUDIO FAULT is visual only, because a sound could not play (Rev 3.28).

- Audio owner (Proposed): with several tabs open only one plays sound. The tab that clicked Start monitoring takes a Web Lock and the others show "Sound in another tab". If the owner closes, another tab shows SIREN OFF: click to enable (browser audio is the NO LIVE DATA siren only, 3.12).

- Sound on the audio clock (Proposed): the browser siren (NO LIVE DATA only) is a continuous loop on the Web Audio clock; Silence 60 s stops it. Page timers are throttled in a hidden or minimized tab, so they are never used to repeat a sound.

- Repeat alerts (Proposed): the same alert type on the same responder raised again while its earlier card is still on screen joins that card (a count shows) and does not stack a new one. After Acknowledge, a new raise starts a new card and sounds.

- Responder selection (Proposed): a new alert never switches the selected jacket by itself. The responder's switcher entry flashes, and clicking a card selects that responder.

- Acknowledge is stored on the host (Proposed): it is saved on the alert (acknowledged_ts and operator), written to audit_events and sent in the snapshot. A reload, a second tab or a host restart therefore never makes an acknowledged alert sound again or loses it, and every tab shows the same state. The click silences the card at once on screen. The call to the host is sent in the background and retried, and the card shows "ack pending" until the host confirms. A failed call never un-silences the card. Acknowledging an episode also acknowledges its alerts.

- Man-down and MAYDAY cards have two steps: Acknowledge (stops the sound), then Resolve, which needs the node, episode ID and a reason (rescued, false alarm, device removed). Neither clears the jacket latch: only the jacket CANCEL does (errata 3). Other cards have Acknowledge only. For MAN_DOWN (not MAYDAY), chest movement >STILL_G for 5 s changes the card text to "MAN-DOWN (movement detected)" (card, siren and DANGER level never reduce), and 15 s of renewed stillness reverts the label back to "MAN-DOWN: no response" (Spec §7); each 5 s / 15 s transition emits an incremental episode WebSocket frame carrying man_down_motion_state ("movement_detected" | "no_response") so all connected tabs stay synchronized.

- Minimize (Proposed): a card collapses to a chip in the top strip (alias, level, count, elapsed time) and reopens on click. A chip stays until the alert clears or is resolved. A threshold alert that clears before anyone acknowledges it stays as "cleared" until acknowledged, so a short spike is never missed.

- The ML fall or motion classifier is Tier 3. If it is ever built it is advisory only and the deterministic man-down rule stays authoritative.

- Forecast, early-warning and advisory notices never set, delay or clear a safety alert (Spec).
