### 3.6 Look and accessibility (Proposed)

- Colours: page #F7F8FA, cards white with a #E5E7EB border, text #111827. Normal #15803D with white text, warning #FACC15 with dark text, danger #B91C1C with white text. Stale or unreliable: #6B7280 text on a #E5E7EB fill. Buttons are dark slate, so no other hue competes with the levels. These pairs meet 4.5:1 contrast or better (checked).

- Type and space: Inter, bundled with the app so it works offline. Readings 32 px with fixed-width numerals, alert titles 20 px bold, body 16 px, nothing under 14 px. 8 px spacing grid, 14 px corners, soft shadow.

- Not colour alone: every level has an icon and a word (OK, WARNING, DANGER), so the screen still works in greyscale and for colour-blind viewers. Green and yellow are the pair most often confused.

- Flash and motion: the flash is a 1 Hz border pulse, under 3 flashes per second. With the reduced-motion setting it becomes a steady thick border.

- Smoke and gloves: buttons at least 44 px high, alert text is the largest on the screen, and Acknowledge and Resolve work from the keyboard.

- Few widgets (decided): the side panel has at most 8 rows and one chart. The 8 rows: CO; temperature and humidity; chest motion and stillness; battery; SCBA; link and last seen; HR and COHb status ("HR not fitted" in Tier 1, COHb "unavailable"); Details (posture, RSSI, SNR, collapsed). Node level is the card colour and word, not a row. In Tier 2 the CO forecast joins the CO row. No decorative gauges or extra panels.
