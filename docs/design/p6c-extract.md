# P6c extract: 14.1 intro, sensor_fault row, co_threshold row (for the hold and re-evaluate rules), 14.2, GV-07, GV-15, GV-16, GV-19

Verbatim extract of the frozen design (Rev 3.45). The design wins if anything here disagrees with it. Do not edit.

## 14.1 (intro paragraph)

14.1 Alert Lifecycle & Deterministic Rule State Table: Every rule runs on fresh verified frames (verify == 'OK' and seq_class in {'fresh', 'reboot'}) using the injected Clock(wall_ms, mono_ms). Raise-only rule (Rev 3.22): a frame with verify == 'OK' and seq_class == 'suspect' (flags level not 3) is also evaluated, but only for instantaneous triggers that raise or escalate: man_down and mayday (open or upgrade), co_threshold and scba_expired (open or escalate), sensor_fault (open or escalate), node_danger (open), battery_low (open) and checkin (open). A suspect frame never clears, lowers or de-escalates an alert, episode or level; never starts, advances or resets a duration or hysteresis timer (the clear timers, the heat timers, the co_range timer, the 15 s and 60 s silence timers and the shadow estimator); never counts toward link recovery; and never moves the baseline. The displayed node level is the maximum of the current level and the level the suspect frame produces. Frames with verify other than 'OK' and frames whose flags level is 3 are never evaluated (unauth_alert, GV-04, is the one exception). Level-escalating alerts (co_threshold, scba_expired and sensor_fault; sensor_fault is never lowered in place and clears when its fault bits clear) mutate their open alert row's level in place (resetting acknowledged_ts = NULL when escalating WARNING -> DANGER so the siren sounds) rather than creating duplicate cards (idx_alerts_one_open_per_node_type enforces one open row per node and type across all sessions, Section 11). De-escalation (Rev 3.20, Proposed) uses the same row: when the DANGER clear condition has held, the level is lowered to WARNING in place (acknowledged_ts is kept, so no new siren and no new card); when the WARNING clear condition has held, the alert is cleared (cleared_ts set), also directly from DANGER; a later re-escalation resets acknowledged_ts = NULL. The 10 s hysteresis timers count on fresh valid frames only and restart when the value rises back above the clear level. signal_lost (15 s silent after non-adverse frame) clears automatically when link_lost triggers at 60 s or when a fresh frame arrives.

## 14.1 sensor_fault

Rule table row (cells in order: rule, trigger, cardinality and escalation, clear or reset):

sensor_fault

status2 bits 4/5/6 (CO/IMU/temp fault), no valid CO for 5 s, or wrist lost while mated. Level is DANGER (Rev 3.22; Summary 4 and D5) when, on the first frame that shows the fault, either (i) the CO fault or CO timeout comes within sensor_fault_danger_window_s = 60 s after the last valid CO of 35 ppm or more with SCBA OFF (last_co_ge_warn_mono, set on every such frame), or (ii) the temperature fault arrives while a heat alert is open, or within 60 s after one was last open (last_heat_alert_mono, set on every frame while a heat alert is open; any heat WARNING, including extreme, counts). Otherwise WARNING.

1 alert per node, WARNING or DANGER. The level is decided on the first frame that shows the fault and a DANGER onset is never lowered while any fault bit stays set; a WARNING sensor_fault escalates in place (acknowledged_ts = NULL, siren) if a later fault of another kind meets (i) or (ii). Holds the prior CO/heat alert level; never displays 0 ppm. The two reading times are part of the per-node state; the recovery rebuild restores them from the last 30 minutes of frames and the open alert rows (checkpoints are dormant, 2.10).

Clears when all fault bits == 0 and valid readings resume. After it clears, co_threshold and heat re-evaluate from the next fresh valid frame only, never from the frame that cleared the fault.

## 14.1 co_threshold (context)

Rule table row (cells in order: rule, trigger, cardinality and escalation, clear or reset):

co_threshold

SCBA OFF (id_mode bit 3 == 0) & a usable CO reading (14.2: VALID, DERATED, or UNRELIABLE only because of co_range or sht40_fault; a CO fault or CO timeout has no reading): >=35 ppm (WARNING), >=200 ppm or co_sat (DANGER)

1 open alert per node; escalates WARNING->DANGER in place (clears acknowledged_ts); de-escalates DANGER->WARNING in place (keeps acknowledged_ts)

DANGER lowers to WARNING after 10 s continuous below 180 ppm while CO is still at or above 31.5 ppm. The alert clears after 10 s continuous below 31.5 ppm (from WARNING or DANGER; if both timers have expired it clears). Suppressed (not cleared) when SCBA ON. Held (not lowered, not cleared, timers not running) while co_range is active or the SHT40 is faulted; the 10 s timers start at the first fresh frame after the hold ends (14.2)

## 14.2

14.2 CO & SHT40 Validity Classes, co_range, Hold Rules & Shadow Estimator (Rev 3.22): (1) CO validity. UNRELIABLE when any of: status2 bit 4 == 1 (CO fault); no valid CO frame for more than co_fault_timeout_s (5 s); co_range is active (2); or the SHT40 has a fault (status2 bit 6 == 1), because CO validity cannot then be judged. UNRELIABLE shows the last valid CO in grey as 'CO unreliable' (never 0 ppm), with the reason co_fault, co_timeout, co_range or sht40_fault. DERATED during the 210 s warm-up (status3 bit 2 == 1): show the co_warmup badge and keep the thresholds active. VALID otherwise (a value above 500 ppm sets co_over_range = 1 and clamps the display to 500.0 ppm while the raw co_x10 is stored). (2) co_range (Summary 2 and 7, H5, D5, D6). It is judged from the SHT40 in the same frame (the sensor sits beside the vent and the CO sensor body lags the air), only while the SHT40 is healthy. It becomes active on the first fresh frame with temp_x100 > co_range_temp_max_c x 100 (55 C), temp_x100 < co_range_temp_min_c x 100 (-10 C), hum < co_range_rh_min_pct (15) or hum > co_range_rh_max_pct (90); the limits themselves are inside the range. It clears only after co_range_clear_s = 60 s of fresh frames continuously inside the range narrowed by the margins: temp_c from -10 + co_range_margin_c = -5 C up to 55 - 5 = 50 C, and hum from 15 + co_range_margin_rh_pct = 20% up to 90 - 5 = 85%. A fresh frame outside that band restarts the 60 s count; suspect frames neither count nor restart it. co_range_temp_max_c is the UNRELIABLE limit: 55 C (T_derate) until D6 sets it; if D6 raises it, readings between 55 C and the new limit are DERATED (low confidence, no hold). (3) Hold rule. While CO is UNRELIABLE for co_range or sht40_fault, any open co_threshold level, and any CO-driven scba_expired level, is held: it is not lowered and not cleared, and its 10 s hysteresis timers (14.1) do not run (a running timer is reset). When the cause ends (co_range cleared after its 60 s, or status2 bit 6 == 0 on a fresh frame) the normal clear rule must still be met: the 10 s timers start at the first fresh frame after the hold ends, and the time spent held does not count. The raw co_x10 of a fresh frame at or above 35 ppm or 200 ppm during a co_range or sht40_fault hold still opens the alert (when none is open) or escalates it, even though its validity is UNRELIABLE (a high reading is never hidden; GV-18 g); only lowering and clearing are held. A CO fault or CO timeout is different: it has no usable reading, so it opens no co_threshold and raises sensor_fault (GV-07). A CO fault or CO timeout holds the level the same way. co_range raises no card of its own. The node object in the snapshot and in every reading carries co_validity (VALID, DERATED or UNRELIABLE) and co_reason (null, co_fault, co_timeout, co_range or sht40_fault); the node tile shows them and frames.reasons records them. While a node with an open co_threshold alert has co_validity UNRELIABLE and co_reason co_range or sht40_fault, the CO card shows the label 'Held: temperature or humidity out of range' or 'Held: temperature sensor fault' beside its level, so an operator can see why it does not clear. Operational note: a heat WARNING needs 60 C or more, which is above the 55 C limit, so a CO alert raised during a real heat exposure stays held until the SHT40 has been inside the narrowed band for 60 s (adviser to confirm). (4) SHT40 hold for heat: if status2 bit 6 == 1 (temp fault), freeze heat evaluation and hold any active heat alert until the SHT40 recovers. (5) Shadow Estimator: tracks continuous stillness (motion_g <= still_g_default) across fresh frames; ignores disagreements shorter than shadow_grace_s (5.5 s) + shadow_disagree_s (6.0 s), suspends during IMU fault, and requires rebaseline_clean_frames = 3 fresh frames after link recovery.

## Golden vectors

GV-07
Node 1, id_mode=0x41, co_x10=520 on every frame, fresh frames 2 s apart. (a) status2=0x10 (CO fault), flags=0x08 (fault bit). (b) status2=0x00, flags=0x01. (c) status2=0x00, flags=0x01.
(a) co_fault=1, CO UNRELIABLE. (b) all fault bits 0, co_ppm=52.0, node_level=WARNING. (c) co_ppm=52.0, node_level=WARNING.
(a) raises sensor_fault (WARNING); no co_threshold evaluation. (b) clears sensor_fault; co_threshold is NOT raised from this frame; final_level=WARNING (from node_level only). (c) raises co_threshold (WARNING).

GV-15
Node 1, id_mode=0x41, SCBA OFF, fresh frames 2 s apart. (a) co_x10=400 (40 ppm), flags=0x01. (b) 20 s later status2=0x10 (CO fault), flags=0x0A (level DANGER + fault bit). Separate run: (c) co_x10=100 (10 ppm), flags=0x00, then 20 s later status2=0x10, flags=0x09 (level WARNING + fault bit). Separate run: (d) as (a), then frames with co_x10=100 and flags=0x00 every 2 s for 70 s, then status2=0x10, flags=0x09.
(b) co_fault=1, node_level=DANGER, 20 s after a valid CO of 40.0 ppm. (c) co_fault=1, node_level=WARNING, last valid CO 10.0 ppm. (d) co_fault=1, node_level=WARNING, more than 60 s after the last valid CO of 40.0 ppm.
(a) raises co_threshold (WARNING). (b) raises sensor_fault at DANGER (siren; the 60 s window is met), the co_threshold alert stays open and held, final_level=DANGER, no node_danger (a DANGER alert exists). (c) raises sensor_fault at WARNING (beep). (d) raises sensor_fault at WARNING (beep): the 60 s window has passed.

GV-16
Node 1, id_mode=0x41, fresh frames 2 s apart. (a) temp_x100=6500 (65 C) on every frame for 122 s, so heat is open at WARNING (co_range is also active above 55 C), then 30 s later status2=0x40 (temperature fault), flags=0x0A (level DANGER + fault bit). Separate run: (b) temp_x100=2500 on every frame, then status2=0x40, flags=0x09 (level WARNING + fault bit). Separate run: (c) a heat alert has been open for 10 minutes, then status2=0x40, flags=0x0A.
(a) temp_fault=1, node_level=DANGER, heat alert open. (b) temp_fault=1, node_level=WARNING, no heat alert. (c) temp_fault=1, node_level=DANGER, heat alert open for 10 minutes.
(a) raises sensor_fault at DANGER (siren; the 60 s window is met); the heat alert stays open and held; CO is UNRELIABLE (sht40_fault); no node_danger. (b) raises sensor_fault at WARNING (beep); CO is UNRELIABLE (sht40_fault). (c) raises sensor_fault at DANGER (siren): a heat alert is open at the fault frame.

GV-19
Node 1, id_mode=0x41, SCBA OFF, fresh frames 2 s apart. (a) co_x10=520, flags=0x01. (b) status2=0x40 (temperature fault), flags=0x09, co_x10=200 (20 ppm) for 30 s. (c) status2=0x00, flags=0x00, co_x10=200 for 20 s.
(b) temp_fault=1, node_level=WARNING, co_ppm=20.0. (c) all fault bits 0, co_ppm=20.0.
(a) raises co_threshold (WARNING). (b) raises sensor_fault (WARNING; no heat alert, so no DANGER); the co_threshold alert stays open and held, CO shown low confidence, no clear timer runs. (c) the first healthy frame clears sensor_fault; the co_threshold clear timer starts at the next fresh frame and the alert clears after 10 s continuous below 31.5 ppm.
